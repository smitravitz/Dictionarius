# Privacy policy

_Dictionarius, by smitravitz. Last updated: 12 November 2026._

**Dictionarius collects nothing about you.** It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it never sends anything from your device.

## The apps (iPhone and Android)

- The apps never connect to the internet. The whole dictionary is inside the app.
- The Android app asks for no permissions. The iPhone app asks for none either.
- What you type, the words you open and your settings stay on your device, in the app's own storage. Removing the app removes them.
- A link to Wiktionary, to the data's license or to the source code opens in your own browser, and only when you tap it. From there, that site's privacy policy applies.

## The web app

The web app at <https://smitravitz.github.io/Dictionarius/> loads only its own files and sends nothing anywhere. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, and GitHub, like any host, sees the address your device connects from when it downloads the page ([GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)). After the first visit it keeps the dictionary on your device and works offline.

## The stores

If you install from the App Store or Google Play, Apple or Google may record the download and, if you have agreed to it in your device's settings, share crash and usage statistics with developers. That is their service, under their privacy policies; Dictionarius itself adds nothing to it.

## Children

Dictionarius collects nothing from anyone, children included.

## Checking this

The app is open source: <https://github.com/smitravitz/Dictionarius>. [`privacy.toml`](privacy.toml) lists what the app may do, and every build is checked against it.
